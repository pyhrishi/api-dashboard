# Positioning

## One line
**Zinbit by Zintlr — identity infrastructure for the AI era.** The developer API for verified B2B identity: enrich any email, phone, LinkedIn, or domain into a complete, compliant, registry-verified record — in one call.

## The story (how we talk about it)

1. **Two engines, one truth.** A 400M+ contact graph (*Lookup Engine*) for reach, and deterministic government-registry identity (*IDS Engine* — MCA CIN/DIN) for certainty. Where they disagree, **registry wins**. You get breadth *and* verified truth — nobody else gives you both.
2. **Compliance is the default, not a checkbox.** Live traffic is masked per DPDP/GDPR/CCPA automatically, routed to the right region, and honors opt-outs end-to-end. You don't configure compliance — you inherit it.
3. **Usage-based, radically transparent.** Every endpoint shows its credit cost. Every response tells you what it cost and what's left. Forecasts warn you before you run out. No surprise invoices.
4. **Built for the engineer who ships it and the leader who owns it.** A console with replayable logs, trace waterfalls, webhook retries, root-cause analysis, key scopes — and the audit, RBAC, residency, and cost controls the enterprise needs to say yes.
5. **A platform, not just an API.** Partners resell it, warehouses share it zero-copy, teams grow into it.

## Proof points we lead with
- 120ms p99 · 99.99% SLA tiers · 16 endpoints across people, companies, identity, directors
- DPDP/GDPR/CCPA native masking · data-residency (US/EU/IN) · opt-out propagation
- Registry-verified identity (MCA) — a category incumbents don't have
- Self-serve sandbox → first successful call in minutes → usage-based scale

## Tone
Confident, precise, developer-respectful. Show, don't claim — live demos, real payloads, real headers. Never marketing fluff over a substantive screen.

## Anti-positioning (what we are not)
Not a sales-rep CRM/sequencer (Apollo). Not a Chrome extension (Lusha). Not a sales-led enterprise contract you can't try (ZoomInfo). Not a US-only firmographics feed (Clearbit).
